# Dragon Block: Tenkaichi 0.3.0

## Added

### Story mode: the Saiyan Saga
- The whole **Saiyan Saga** of Dragon Ball Z, told from your point of view: you take Goku's place in an alternate timeline, with Gohan as your young student.
- Ten story missions that follow the canon events in order:
  1. **A Peaceful Reunion**: visiting Kame House, where Raditz arrives and kidnaps Gohan;
  2. **An Unlikely Alliance**: teaming up with Piccolo against Raditz, Gohan's outburst and the Special Beam Cannon;
  3. **Snake Way**: King Yemma's check-in station and the long run to King Kai's planet;
  4. **Catch Bubbles** and 5. **Hit Gregory**, under ten times Earth's gravity;
  6. **King Kai's Techniques**: learning the **Kaioken** and the **Spirit Bomb**;
  7. **Back to Life**: the wish, the run back along Snake Way, the Saiyans landing on Earth (Saibamen, Yamcha, Chiaotzu, Tien, Piccolo's sacrifice);
  8. **Nappa**: arriving on the battlefield and defeating Nappa;
  9. **The Prince of Saiyans**: the fight with Vegeta, the Galick Gun against Kamehameha clash with Kaioken x3, the **Power Ball** and **Great Ape Vegeta**, Yajirobe cutting his tail, the Spirit Bomb thrown by Krillin and Gohan's Great Ape transformation;
  10. **After the Storm**: back to Kame House, with Namek on the horizon.
- The story is told mostly through **cutscenes** taken from the series, with camera shots, effects and **subtitles** for every line (no voice acting). A cutscene you have already watched can be skipped with **Enter**.
- Story characters with their own looks and fighting styles: Gohan, Krillin, Master Roshi, Bulma, Raditz, Piccolo, Nappa, Vegeta (with scouter), Yamcha, Tien, Chiaotzu, Yajirobe, King Kai, Bubbles, Gregory, King Yemma, Kami and the Saibamen.
- Story fights against Raditz, Nappa and Vegeta (also as a Great Ape), with allies fighting at your side.
- New story locations: Raditz's landing field and the Saiyan battlefield with their pods, built once per world.
- **The Other World**, a new dimension under a golden sky: King Yemma's check-in station, the winding **Snake Way** (falling off puts you back on the road) and **King Kai's planet** with his house, car and tree. On King Kai's planet the gravity is ten times heavier until you complete his training; a halo floats over your head while you are dead.
- The story starts automatically for new characters; existing characters can start it from the Missions menu.

### Missions
- New **mission system** for both story and side missions.
- **Missions menu** (**O**): active missions with progress, missions you can accept with their rewards and requirements, tracking, abandoning side missions and the completed count.
- **Mission HUD** on the right side of the screen with the tracked mission, its current objective, progress and the distance to the target.
- Optional **world marker** that always points to the exact spot to reach (or the enemy to fight), with arrows at the screen edge when it is out of view. It can be turned off from the Missions menu.
- Side missions: **Raider Hunt**, **The Sacred Water**, **Lunch for the Master** (cooked cod for Master Roshi), **It's Over 9000!**, **The Room of Spirit and Time** and **Turtle School Training**, with Training Point rewards; repeatable once completed.

### Racial mechanics
- **Ki sense** (**I**): feel the ki of nearby fighters, players and creatures, shown on screen with their relative strength (▲). The range grows with Ki Control and Spirit.
  - **Androids** cannot be sensed from afar, only up close.
  - **God Ki** (Kaioshin and divine forms) cannot be sensed by mortals and cannot be read by scouters.
- **Scouter**: a new wearable item that reads exact power levels and shows a scouter overlay; it explodes when it reads a power level that is too high. Reading a power level over 9000 has its own reaction.
- **Saiyan tail**:
  - it can be wrapped around the waist (character menu) so it cannot be grabbed;
  - a grabbed tail paralyses an untrained Saiyan;
  - it can be cut off by some techniques and grows back after a while.
- **Great Ape**: a Saiyan with a tail who looks at the **full moon** at night, or at a **Power Ball**, turns into a giant Great Ape; it ends when the moon is out of sight or the tail is cut off.
- **Absorption** for Majin and Bio-Androids: absorbing a weakened fighter heals you and permanently adds a share of power; absorptions unlock the absorption forms.

### Transformations
- New canonical transformations for every race, manga forms included, each with its own trial arena and **extra passives** shown in the Forms menu:
  - **Saiyan**: Great Ape, Ascended Super Saiyan, Ultra Super Saiyan, Full Power Super Saiyan, Super Saiyan 4, Wrathful, Legendary Super Saiyan, Super Saiyan God, Super Saiyan Blue, Super Saiyan Blue Evolved, Super Saiyan Rosé, Ultra Instinct -Sign-, Ultra Instinct, True Ultra Instinct and Ultra Ego.
  - **Half-Saiyan**: Super Saiyan, its Grade 2/3 and Full Power variants, Super Saiyan 2, Super Saiyan Rage, Ultimate and Beast.
  - **Human**: Potential Unlocked and Max Power.
  - **Namekian**: Super Namekian, Great Namek, Orange Piccolo and Orange Giant.
  - **Frieza race**: Second, Third and Final Form, 100% Full Power, Golden Frieza and Black Frieza.
  - **Majin**: Super Buu, Super Buu with Gotenks absorbed, Super Buu with Gohan absorbed, and Kid Buu.
  - **Bio-Android**: Semi-Perfect, Perfect and Super Perfect Form.
  - **Kaioshin**: Fused Zamasu and Half-Corrupted Zamasu.
  - **Demon**: Third Eye.
  - Androids have no canon transformations; they rely on their infinite energy.
- Passives make every form play differently, for example:
  - **Super Saiyan God** is lean and fast: more speed, much lower ki drain, health regeneration and God Ki;
  - **Super Saiyan Blue** hits harder but drains more ki;
  - **Ultra Instinct** dodges attacks on its own and deflects ki blasts;
  - **Ultra Ego** grows stronger with every hit it takes and burns the opponent's ki;
  - **Great Ape**, **Great Namek** and **Orange Giant** are giant and cannot be knocked back; the Great Ape and the Wrathful forms rampage until you master them.
- Branching forms: when a form leads to several others, set your **Target** in the Forms menu and the transform key climbs toward it.
- Every transformation has its own **canonical cutscene** (or an original one in the same style when the series has none).
- New looks: Great Ape body with fur, muzzle and glowing red eyes, Super Saiyan 4 red fur and mane, Beast's tall white spire, Frieza's horned, elongated and smooth forms, and more.

### Techniques
- New canonical techniques, bought from the Techniques tab like the others:
  - **Saiyan**: Double Sunday, Bomber DX, Power Ball, Hakai (Ultra Ego);
  - **Human**: Wolf Fang Fist, Dodon Ray, Solar Flare, Spirit Ball, Destructo Disc, Telekinesis, Scattering Bullet, Tri-Beam;
  - **Namekian**: Light Grenade, Hellzone Grenade;
  - **Frieza race**: Death Saucer, Death Ball;
  - **Majin**: Candy Beam, Absorption, Vanishing Ball, Innocence Cannon, Human Extinction Attack, Planet Burst;
  - **Android**: Eye Laser, Power Blitz, Energy Absorption, Barrier, Hell's Flash;
  - **Bio-Android**: Perfect Kamehameha, Absorption, Barrier, Cell Juniors;
  - **Kaioshin**: Restoration, Kai Kai, Telekinesis, Lightning of Absolution (Fused Zamasu);
  - **Demon**: Evil Impulse, Stone Spit;
  - **Everyone**: Instant Transmission; the **Spirit Bomb** is learned in the story from King Kai.
- New kinds of techniques: giant charged spheres raised above the head (Spirit Bomb, Death Ball, Planet Burst), cutting discs, lasers, barrages that surround or rain on the target, and support techniques (blinding, teleporting, healing, barriers, paralysis, petrification, absorption, summoning).
- New animations for discs, raised spheres, the Solar Flare, barriers, throws and grabs.

## Changed
- The **Voice** option has been removed from character creation.
- The Forms menu shows each form's passives, its parent form and every requirement (tail, absorptions, full moon, story progress).

## Fixed
- Story characters hidden during a cutscene are no longer drawn.
