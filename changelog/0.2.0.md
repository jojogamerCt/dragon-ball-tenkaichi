# Dragon Block: Tenkaichi 0.2.0

## Added

### Transformations
- **Super Saiyan 2**: transform from Super Saiyan once your base Battle Power reaches 60,000; lightning aura and sharper spikes.
- **Super Saiyan 3**: transform from Super Saiyan 2 once your base Battle Power reaches 200,000; long flowing hair and lightning aura.
- **Kaioken**: a separate power-up that every race can use, stackable on top of compatible forms (base and Super Saiyan).
  - Levels x2, x3, x4, x10 and x20, unlocked as your Kaioken mastery grows.
  - Press **Y** to raise the level; after the highest unlocked level it turns off. Power down (B) also releases it.
  - Multiplies Battle Power, damage and speed, but drains Ki and health; it breaks off when your body cannot take the strain.
  - Red-tinted aura and a Kaioken indicator on the HUD.
  - Forms can declare compatibility through the new datapack field `kaiokenCompatible`.

### Techniques
- New techniques: **Galick Gun**, **Masenko**, **Special Beam Cannon**, **Final Flash**, **Big Bang Attack** and **Death Beam**.
- Every technique has its own canonical animation, in third and first person, and its charge glow appears where the hands are:
  - **Kamehameha**: hands cupped at the side with the body turned;
  - **Galick Gun**: body turned the other way with both arms drawn back, then both palms forward;
  - **Masenko**: both hands raised above the head, then thrust forward from above;
  - **Special Beam Cannon**: two fingers to the forehead, then one arm pointed at the target; the beam is a thin core wrapped in a spinning spiral, as in the anime;
  - **Final Flash**: arms spread wide, then brought together in front;
  - **Big Bang Attack**: one open palm thrust forward;
  - **Death Beam**: a quick one-finger point;
  - **Ki Blast**: alternating palms.
- Techniques are now learned by **buying** them with Training Points or from **Masters**, never automatically.
  - Racial techniques and the Kaioken can be bought from the Techniques tab of the character menu, once your base Battle Power is high enough.

### Masters, lessons and trials
- Canonical Masters: **Master Roshi**, **Korin** and **Kami**, each with their own look (Korin is a small, cat-sized white cat with ears, muzzle and tail).
- Talking to a Master opens a lessons menu: buy a lesson with Training Points or take the Master’s **trial**.
  - **Master Roshi**: Kamehameha.
  - **Korin**: Senzu Beans and the Sacred Water trial (+2 Speed, Agility and Martial Arts).
  - **Kami**: the Hyperbolic Time Chamber, once per day (+250 TP).
- **Trials** work like in Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2:
  - you are teleported to a training realm built for the occasion (rocky wasteland, Kame Island, top of Korin Tower, Hyperbolic Time Chamber);
  - after a countdown the fight begins, with a time limit;
  - win or lose, you are sent back to the exact spot where the trial started, even after a disconnect.

### Iconic locations
- Generated once per world near spawn, each with its Master:
  - **Kame House** on its own island in the nearest ocean;
  - **Korin Tower**, a tall pillar with Korin’s sanctuary at the top;
  - **Kami’s Lookout**, floating above the tower, with the palace, towers, trees and the red-patterned rim.
- `/tenkaichi landmarks` shows where they are.

### Commands
- `/tenkaichi landmarks` (everyone), `/tenkaichi trial start|end` (operators).
- `/tenkaichi learn` also accepts the Kaioken.

## Changed
- Auras are much more visible: the flames always face the camera and are strongest around the body, so they no longer look like thin transparent stripes.
- The Martial Arts Master menu now lists lessons with their price, requirements and trial button.
- Racial techniques are no longer unlocked automatically by Battle Power.
- Outfits no longer have the white round emblem on the chest.
